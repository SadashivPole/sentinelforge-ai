About SentinelForge

An open prototype detection engineering platform for SOC teams.

What this is

SentinelForge AI is a demonstrable SOC detection engineering platform built in Python.

It is not a production SIEM, not a hosted service.

Design principles

  • Evidence-first: every alert cites the exact raw log line(s).
  • No fabrication: no invented indicators, no auto remediation.
  • Local-first: SQLite on local host, no telemetry upload.
  • Human in the loop: agent surfaces findings; humans act.
